Today i wanted to talk about Favicons! Favicons are the little icons that show up in the browser tab when you visit a website. they are usually 16x16 pixels or 32x32 pixels. They usually are logos of a company or website. take a look at mine. mine is catmelon, the mascot of blaha din din din

Favicons are really easy to make! you can use any image editing software to make one. just make sure to save it as a .ico file. you can use online converters to convert png or jpg files to ico files.
once you have your favicon.ico file, you just need to upload it to the root directory of your website. then, you need to add the following code to the head section of your HTML file:
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.ico" type="image/x-icon">
thats it! now when you visit your website, you should see your favicon in the browser tab.
Favicons are cool. however. i just learned today favicons can be animated! yes its true! you can make animated favicons using .gif files. however, not all browsers support animated favicons. but its still a cool feature to have!
Animated favicons are still a new thing. so i recommend just using icos. but if you want to try animated favicons, go ahead! just make sure to test it on different browsers to see if it works.
